# Poker chip recordings

Source: [Casino Audio 1.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio) by Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl).
License: CC0 1.0; the original license is included in LICENSE.txt.

The 19 chip Foley recordings are converted from the pack's OGG files to mono
44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV for browser compatibility, including iPhones. Leading
and trailing silence is trimmed with a small edge fade, and peaks are leveled.
The recording's pitch, contact timing, and frequency response are preserved.

- `lay-*.wav`: placing an individual chip
- `collide-*.wav`: chip-to-chip impacts
- `handle-*.wav`: moving and handling chips
- `stack-*.wav`: returning chips to a stack

Files are served locally by the laptop; no external audio service is used.
